Authorial approach

Why these stories?

I write about people pushed into choices they cannot undo.

Genre is my engine, not my destination. Whether the story becomes a supernatural thriller, a geopolitical drama or a family dramedy, I start with pressure: a character wants something, the world resists, and every decision carries a cost.

I want African realities to be specific on screen without becoming closed worlds — stories that feel rooted in where they come from and still travel.
