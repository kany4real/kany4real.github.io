Signature series · In development

La Veine Bleue

8 × 52 min · Geopolitical thriller · Moral drama · Premium series

Sent to audit a powerful mining group, an Ivorian lawyer discovers the human and political cost hidden behind its compliance reports — and must decide how much of the system she is willing to destroy to expose the truth.

The story

Amara Diop has built her career inside systems that know how to turn responsibility into procedure. When she is sent to the Democratic Republic of Congo to audit the operations of a major mining group, she expects another difficult assignment and another step toward the career she has been promised.

Instead, the audit begins to expose contradictions between what the company reports, what local communities experience and what powerful interests need to remain invisible. The deeper Amara goes, the less useful neutrality becomes. Her professional mandate gradually turns into a personal and political choice.

The world

La Veine Bleue enters the world of strategic minerals, corporate compliance and political influence through the people who are expected to make an opaque system look clean.

The series moves between corporate offices, mine sites, political networks and communities whose lives are directly shaped by decisions made far away. The cobalt economy is not background: it is the pressure point connecting money, technology, sovereignty and human lives.

The dramatic engine

Every revelation creates a new moral cost. The closer Amara gets to the truth, the more her professional position becomes a weapon that can be used against her — and the more difficult it becomes to pretend that the problem is simply the misconduct of a few individuals.

The series operates on two levels: the immediate suspense of an investigation and the longer game of power around resources, reputation and political survival. The central question is not only who is responsible? but what happens when telling the truth threatens the entire structure that gave you access to it?

Amara Diop

Amara is not an outsider looking in. Her strength comes from understanding the language and mechanisms of the system she is investigating. That is also what makes her vulnerable: she has something to lose.

Her arc is the movement from professional compliance to personal responsibility — from believing that systems can be corrected from within to deciding what she is prepared to risk when correction is no longer enough.

Series ambition

The first season is conceived as a complete investigative and moral arc while opening a larger world capable of sustaining further seasons. The ambition is to combine the propulsion of a contemporary thriller with the complexity of a political drama rooted in African realities and connected to global stakes.
