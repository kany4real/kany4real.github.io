Sortie de Route

8 × 52 min · Supernatural thriller · African tech-noir · Original series

In a hyperconnected city, a ride-hailing driver and a disgraced inspector are drawn into a chain of impossible deaths where reflections, screens and an unseen digital presence begin to rewrite reality.

Original seriesMOVE · Red Noise · N'DRI

Logline

Ajara makes a living behind the wheel, navigating a city where movement, money and reputation are increasingly mediated by the MOVE app. When a death that should be impossible refuses to stay buried, she crosses paths with Kouakou, a disgraced inspector. Their investigation opens onto glitches, reflections and traces that disappear — and a phenomenon connected to the N'DRI lineage.

The story

The series begins with a disturbance that does not fit the official explanation. What looks like an isolated death becomes the first visible crack in a larger system. Ajara's mobility puts her in contact with places, people and digital traces that others overlook, while Kouakou brings investigative instincts and institutional baggage.

As their paths converge, the city itself becomes unstable: screens, mirrors, phones and other ordinary surfaces begin behaving as if they can carry something that does not belong there. The investigation moves from accident to pattern, from pattern to lineage, and from lineage to a threat that changes the meaning of reality.

MOVE

MOVE is part of the series' urban infrastructure: a ride-hailing platform through which people, money, data and movement circulate. It is not simply a technological prop. It gives the investigation access to the city's hidden geography and becomes one of the surfaces through which the mystery can spread.

Red Noise

What first appears to be a collection of glitches gradually points toward Red Noise, a larger phenomenon whose meaning expands beyond the initial investigation. The season uses it as a progressive mystery rather than an answer delivered upfront.

N'DRI

The N'DRI lineage connects the contemporary supernatural disturbance to a deeper history. Family memory, inherited secrets and the disappearance left by Koffi make the mythology personal rather than purely conceptual.

The dramatic engine

Every episode advances two investigations: the immediate mystery of an anomaly or death, and the deeper question of what is happening to the city itself. Each answer changes the meaning of what came before, pushing the characters toward a reality they can no longer explain away.

Ajara

Practical, mobile and difficult to intimidate, Ajara is an unwilling witness to a city changing beneath its own infrastructure. Her position behind the wheel places her at the intersection of the visible city and its hidden movements.

Kouakou

A disgraced inspector, Kouakou brings institutional knowledge and investigative instincts to the partnership. His usefulness is inseparable from the reasons he no longer belongs inside the system.

Series ambition

Sortie de Route uses contemporary African urban life as the foundation for a supernatural mythology rather than treating the setting as exotic decoration. Technology, family history, social memory and the fear of losing control over what is real converge inside a thriller designed around progressive revelation.
