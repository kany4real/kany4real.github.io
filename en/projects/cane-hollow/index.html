Cane Hollow

Supernatural horror · Family drama · 95–105 min · English

A thirteen-year-old girl who records everything is pulled with her fractured blended family into Cane Hollow, a Louisiana town that should not exist — and a house that survives by erasing strangers from memory.

The film

Cane Hollow is a supernatural horror story built around a fractured family, a town sustained by sacrifice and a house that does more than kill: it changes the evidence that someone ever existed.

The story uses Tea’s camera and the family’s attempts to reconstruct reality as both narrative engine and emotional core.

FormatFeature filmGenreSupernatural horror · Family dramaRuntime95–105 minLanguageEnglishRoleWriter

The premise

Tea Hills is thirteen and records everything because footage protects her from adults rewriting what happened. On a road trip to Austin, glitches lead her family to Cane Hollow. At the Caron House, six places are already waiting.

The central rule

The house does not simply take lives. It attacks the traces that connect a person to the world: names, memories, images, objects and the spaces they occupied.

The authorial angle

The horror is inseparable from a family drama about truth, denial and the fear that memory can be manipulated. Tea’s need to keep proof becomes the emotional opposite of a house designed to erase proof.
